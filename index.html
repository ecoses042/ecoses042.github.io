Biography

Jiwoo Kum is a M.S. student in the School of Software at Soongsil University. He is currently conducting research at the Natural Language Processing Lab. His research interests include natural language processing (NLP) and large language models (LLMs), with a particular focus on understanding and analyzing the internal mechanisms and behaviors of LLMs. His current research explores the interpretability and analysis of large language models.
